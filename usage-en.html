02

Progress, profile and your report

Everything is tracked locally, on the device you're using — nothing is sent to a server.

Your student profile

The first time you open the app you're asked for your name, ID/passport, and university email. It's saved only on this device, used to label your progress report, and locked afterwards — resetting your progress offers to update it.

The progress card

The sidebar always shows how many of the 120 lessons you've completed, with a progress bar. Tap it to open a per-mode breakdown.

View report

From that same breakdown, generates a printable, downloadable page listing every lesson you've passed per mode — handy to hand to an instructor or keep for yourself.

Reset progress

Erases everything saved on this device: completed lessons and your saved position in any lesson in progress. This cannot be undone.

03

Search and quick codes

Two lightweight tools live in the top bar, and both work from anywhere in the app.

Search a command

Looks through the commands from lessons you've already completed — by command, code, or concept. It's a recap tool for what you've studied, not a shortcut past it.

Quick code

Type a city or airport name, or a 3-letter code, to look it up — the same reference a real agent checks with DAN or DAC. It's also available inside every exercise, right next to the answer box.

04

Reading aids inside every lesson

A few small visual cues make each screen faster to read, without changing a single word of the original lesson content.

Codes that explain themselves

A code the lesson already defined earlier — or a recognized city/airport code — gets a dashed underline. Hover it (or tap it on a phone) and its meaning shows up right there.

01 BHPX3M 1534 B @ S01JUN 31OCT - 14 3M M

What the screen is showing you

On a step that's pure explanation — nothing to type — the exact part of the system display being talked about is highlighted, even if that display was produced by an earlier step.

05

Offline and updates

Once installed, GDS Training works with no connection at all, and checks quietly for updates whenever it's opened online.
