Getting GDS Training
onto your device

GDS Training installs like an app straight from your browser — no app store, no download links. Pick your device below, follow the steps, and it'll sit on your home screen or desktop ready to open offline.

eyeguas.github.io/GDS

iPhone & iPad

via Safari

Open Safari and go to eyeguas.github.io/GDS.

It has to be Safari — Chrome and other browsers on iOS can't add the app to your home screen.

Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bottom toolbar (or the top bar on iPad).

Scroll the share menu down and tap Add to Home Screen.

Tap Add in the top-right corner.

A "GDS Training" icon appears on your home screen. Open it from there, not from Safari — that's what makes it work offline and feel like a real app.

✓

Once it's installed, open it over Wi-Fi one time so it can save the lessons for offline use. After that, it works with no signal at all.

Android

via Chrome

Open Chrome and go to eyeguas.github.io/GDS.

Chrome usually shows an Install app banner or button at the bottom of the screen. Tap it.

Don't see it? Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner instead.

From that menu, tap Install app (sometimes labelled Add to Home screen).

Confirm by tapping Install.

GDS Training now has its own icon in your app drawer and home screen, and opens in its own window without Chrome's address bar.

✓

Open it once with an internet connection so the lessons are stored on your device — after that it runs fully offline.

Windows

via Chrome or Edge

Open Chrome or Edge and go to eyeguas.github.io/GDS.

Look at the right side of the address bar for a small install icon — a monitor with a down arrow (Chrome) or a similar icon (Edge).

Click it, then click Install in the pop-up.

No icon in the address bar? Open the ⋮ menu and look for Install GDS Training or Apps → Install this site as an app.

GDS Training opens in its own window and gets shortcuts on your Desktop and Start menu, just like any other installed program.

✓

Pin it to your taskbar for one-click access: right-click the app's icon while it's open and choose Pin to taskbar.

Mac

via Chrome, Edge or Safari

Open your browser and go to eyeguas.github.io/GDS.

Chrome or Edge: click the install icon at the right of the address bar, then click Install.

Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer): open the File menu and choose Add to Dock….

Confirm the name and click Add (Safari) or Install (Chrome/Edge).

GDS Training now opens as its own app from the Dock or Launchpad, in its own window.

✓

Older macOS on Safari doesn't support "Add to Dock" — use Chrome or Edge instead, both install the same way as on Windows.

Linux

via Chrome or Edge

Open Chrome or Edge (or another Chromium-based browser) and go to eyeguas.github.io/GDS.

Click the install icon on the right of the address bar. If you don't see one, open the ⋮ menu and choose Install GDS Training….

Click Install in the confirmation dialog.

GDS Training is added to your applications menu and opens in its own window, no browser tabs or address bar.

✓

Firefox on Linux doesn't support installing this kind of app — use Chrome or Edge for the installed, offline-ready version.

Keeping it up to date

GDS Training checks for new lessons and fixes automatically every time you open it with an internet connection, and downloads the update quietly in the background — no button to press, no store to check.

Fully close the app and reopen it once or twice.

A background update needs the app to restart before it takes over — on desktop, close the window entirely (not just switch away); on phone, swipe it away from your recent apps, then reopen.

iPhone / iPad still showing the old version? ›

Rarely, an iPhone or iPad keeps an older copy cached even after reopening a few times. If that happens:

  1. Open Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data.
  2. Find eyeguas.github.io in the list and swipe to delete it.
  3. Go back to your home screen, press and hold the GDS Training icon, and choose Delete App.
  4. Open Safari, go to eyeguas.github.io/GDS again, and re-add it with Add to Home Screen as before.
!
Before clearing website data, know this:

It erases everything the app has saved on that device — your completed lessons, your language choice, and your saved student profile. Only use this fix if reopening the app normally hasn't picked up the update, and expect to redo your profile screen and progress afterward.
